## Text sources

- https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_11155385: 澎湃/全现在 interview with lead artist 千九 (2021-02-06). He says it is "not really 水墨". The art draws on 吴冠中、张大千、潘天寿、戴敦邦、林风眠. Old paintings are eye-level while the game is a 45° top-down view, so elements are pulled out of paintings into one game cell. Gamers like brighter colour than low-saturation 国画.
- https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/9QcyOitSZPHC7wWupw54Zw: CEO 鲍嵬伟 on pre-production. The references are 清明上河图/姑苏繁华图/南都繁会图. The first concept was ink-only (judged too monochrome), the second was a colour version after 吴冠中, and the final style merges them. The concept image was fully hand-painted and its houses were then cut out and put into the engine.
- https://www.boxuu.com/articles/c/10900.html: 葡萄君 reprint (2020-05). The art rules are "重色描线，低饱和颜色，非虚构的细节设计". Each building is painted from one side and mirrored for the other orientation, and characters never show their backs. Story illustrations copy Ming woodblock prints, with ragged edges and colour misregistration.
- https://www.gameres.com/874074.html: 青年报 interview (2020-09). Styles tried included folk prints, paper-cut and a 吴冠中-style ink wash, and 千九's "小鸡啄米图" set the look. 仇英's 清明上河图 sits on the studio shelf.
- https://www.gameres.com/875348.html: CEO interview (2020-10). Art plus content is "七八成" of the game's success, and the team "还在做2D".
- https://paper.people.com.cn/hwbwap/html/2020-08/25/content_2004988.htm: 人民日报海外版 (2020-08-25). Scenes come from 仇英《清明上河图》 and 徐扬《姑苏繁华图》, and the player plays 文徵明 (吴门画派). Adventure illustrations imitate Suzhou woodblock prints with misaligned colour blocks.
- https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20200727A0WDOT00: player research (Taper 夜弓) tracing in-game buildings to 张择端本, 仇英本 and 清院本《清明上河图》, 仇英《独乐园图》 and 南都繁会图.
- https://www.taptap.cn/moment/15218126903837752: official post (2020-05-15). The opening PV is in 螺钿 (mother-of-pearl lacquer) style with stop-motion frame skipping. Styles also tried were tomb murals, Dunhuang murals, woodblock prints and textiles.
- https://jishu.proginn.com/doc/7755647697da108a1: Cocos talk by 椰岛's mini-game lead (2021). Sprites pan in from 水墨色 to 彩色 using one shared material and a per-vertex time parameter. There are 13 render layers, 3 TiledMap ground layers, diamond tiles and road tiles. Background mountains are separate sprites (scaled 0.3). Opening a UI blurs the scene: it is rendered to a small RenderTexture, Kawase-blurred, then upscaled.
- https://blog.csdn.net/wubaohu1314/article/details/120585913: UX analysis. Dragging the map draws line first, then black-and-white ink wash, then full colour, "like a scroll unrolling". The UI has no back/close buttons, the XP bar is hidden in a brush stroke, and progress is shown on painted placards.
- https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cis.jiangnan.googleplay&hl=zh: store text says "美术风格传承吴派山水画，清隽秀逸" and "所有建筑皆出自明朝古画".
- https://m.36kr.com/p/1570749915909763: COO 陈闻 (2022). The game's style is the 吴门画派 style, and 天书奇谭's ink look is "比较接近".
- https://wap.gamersky.com/news/Content-1409880.html: 鸡鸣山 (2021-07-29) is "抬手便可触及夜空的区域", played "在漫漫夏夜的笼罩下".
- https://www.tgbus.com/news/223011: 棕影岛 (4.0.0, 2024). The official text says "昼夜交替": in daytime the waves are emerald, and at night "深蓝静谧的海与岛上的篝火交织".
- https://www.darenjiazu.com/news/202406/163558.html: 篝火大会 is held "夜幕降临时" (2024-07).
