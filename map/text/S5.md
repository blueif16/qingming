## How to read it

- **Station** is metres along the route (`S5.road.main`) from the datum, the centre of the gate passage's outer (east) mouth, between its jamb feet. **+ is east** (picture right, out of the city, toward the bridge); **− is west**. The scroll is read from right to left, so the walk below starts at the east end.
- **Side** is metres north (N, away from the scroll's viewer) or south (S, toward the viewer) of the route's centre line. **H** is height above the ground.
- Directions: E = picture right, N = away from the viewer. "faces W" means the body faces west.
- Positions come from points measured on the strokes. "(est)" marks a point estimated because the strokes hide it (feet behind a rail, a corner behind a tree). Metres also rest on the frame's assumptions below.
- Painted figures are about 1.3× life size against the architecture; positions use foot points, so this doesn't move anyone.

## Open questions

- **Section:** Depth of the tall structures. The ground rate reads the gate block as 9.0 m along the wall, the passage as 2.4 m wide (Song passages are roughly 3-6 m) and 8.3 m tall, and the tower hall as 6.6 m by 3.3 m although it is painted 3 bays by 5. The painter compresses tall structures in depth more than the ground. The strokes fix their positions along the road and their east-west sizes; their north-south sizes are a builder choice, to be recorded in the build notes.
- **Section:** The tax office and the wall. Sources say the office stands against the city wall (名画记: 在街北紧贴城墙的第一家), and it is painted touching the gate block. On the ground its east end is 9.4 m west of the wall's inner face, and it stands north of the gate block (front at D 12.3, block's north end at D 8.5). The wall north of the gate block is not painted, so nothing fixes where its inner face runs there.
- **Section:** The wings (settled in the loyalty pass, 2026-10-11): the gate stands without walls. North of the block only trees and bare ground are painted; the earthen mass below the block's east half is a mound of earth and rock with trees (S5.grd.rock_mound_7200), which scholars read as the slumped earthen wall (土墙杂树). Neither is built as a wall.
- **Section:** The moat. Water is painted only under and beside the bridge. A moat parallel to the wall would cross the open ground north and south of the bridge, where the painting has pines, willows and the bush bank. The bridge's 12.4 m deck spans the water east-west.
- **Section:** The inner ramp (马道). Its flight is painted against the gate block's south face, rising north to the terrace; its foot is below the silk's edge.
- **Section:** The south-side houses. Only their roofs show, cut by the silk's bottom edge; their bases and depths are estimates, and their roof lines slope against the section's receding direction.
