# The tree forms the scroll paints (S4, S5, S6; round 2: S7, S4b, S3, S2, S1 at the end)

These are the 74 mapped trees of S4 (23), S5 (37) and S6 (14), grouped into the forms Zhang Zeduan paints. Positions
come from the map. The forms come from each tree's `what` in `map/world/<S>.json` and from the scan, using the crops
in `gen/trees/look/` and `gen/trees/refs/`. Each form has 1–5 painted cards (`web/public/assets/trees/<form>_<n>.webp`,
listed in `trees.json`). Each tree's card and mirroring are in `assign/<S>.json`. The cards are made by
`tools/tree_cards.mjs` (see `notes.md`). `gen/trees/check/form_<form>.jpg` shows each form's scan reference beside its
cards.

The season is Qingming, early spring. Almost every tree is bare or just budding. The willows carry only a faint haze
of pale green; the pines, the bamboo and one evergreen shrub are the only full greens. The cards use the world's true
colours, not the silk's yellow.

In the list below, (n) is the card variant, m means mirrored, and the height is the mapped painted height.
