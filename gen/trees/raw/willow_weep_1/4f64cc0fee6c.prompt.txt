Image 1 shows the target art style. Image 2 is a detail of the Qingming scroll (清明上河图) showing the kind of tree to paint: take from them only the tree's form, its branching and how its shoots or needles are drawn, and ignore everything else in them (people, roofs, umbrellas, the silk's yellow colour).

Paint ONE single tree in exactly image 1's style: hand-inked dark-brown outlines and linework, light hatching on the trunk, flat muted painted colour fills, crisp clean edges, every shoot drawn as a clear confident stroke.

The tree: an old willow (柳) in early spring as the scroll paints it at the scroll's end, the only willow in the painting whose whips hang. A gnarled, lumpy, knotted trunk with rough grey-brown bark rises and divides into a few crooked limbs; from the limbs and the upper trunk hang long, thin, flexible bare whips in loose curtains, falling straight down like strings, the way a willow's twigs hang before its leaves open. Early spring: the whips are bare, with only tiny swelling buds and the first specks of pale green along them; no leaf masses, no full foliage, no summer green crown; the curtains are see-through, with plenty of sky between the strands.
Colours: grey-brown bark with darker knots; the whips yellow-green to red-brown (about #a6a46a); buds and the first tiny leaves pale willow green (about #a3b56e), sparse.

This one: Like the willow in image 2: a tall gnarled trunk with a knotted lump where its limbs spring, a tall stem rising straight up to the top, and one long limb arching far out to the right and over, with whips hanging from it in a long curtain; curtains of whips fall from every limb; the whole tree taller than it is wide, with the long limb reaching out to the right.

The whole tree from the foot of the trunk to the top of the crown, seen from the front at eye level, centred, filling the height of the picture with a small even margin, nothing cut off by the picture's edge. The trunk ends cleanly at its foot with a slight root flare, standing on nothing.

True natural early-spring daylight colours of the real tree, never the yellowed, darkened silk of the scroll.

Isolated on a solid plain pure white background that fills the whole picture edge to edge: no ground, no grass, no soil patch, no shadow, no mist or glow, no other trees, no other objects, no text, no seal, no border, no frame.
