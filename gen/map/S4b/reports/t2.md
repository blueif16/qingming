## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section", step 1)

> - **Enhance the scan before reading it.** At 2–3×, a contrast-stretched and sharpened crop (sharp
>   `.normalise({lower: 1, upper: 99}).sharpen({sigma: 1})`) shows caps, feet and furniture edges where the raw crop
>   is mush. Use it beside the restyle at the same scale and grid (S4b t2 kept a small `crop.mjs`, with scan, mosaic
>   or both, a 10 px grid and a marks file, in its scratchpad).
> - **Check a crowd count with a marks overlay.** Put your head readings in a marks file, draw them on the enhanced
>   crop and read it again: an unmarked cap is a missed figure, and a mark off a cap is a misread. The crowd round
>   S4b's seated man came to 13, not the 15–20 guessed at zoom 1.
> - **Load or hat:** when a dark mass rises above a figure's head, look for the face beside it and for the mass
>   running down behind the neck. A hat sits on the head; a back load stands behind it. Then compare the painted
>   height both ways with neighbours at the same foot v.
> - **Read an animal's facing from its legs.** The hock (the backward bend) marks the hind legs, so the facing
>   follows even when the head is hidden. On S4b this exposed the restyle's "man bending" as a donkey.
> - **Keep a receding edge that runs the "wrong" way.** S4b's north-side shop furniture recedes up to the left
>   (slopes +0.5 to +1.5 against S4's −1.6). Report the edge with its sign and stroke points; don't drop it as noise.
