## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

> - **Pick the zoom the silk can bear.** On the commons scan, zoom 2–2.5 with grid 20 reads figures best; at zoom
>   3–4 the silk's weave swamps the strokes. Use `tools/scroll_inspect.mjs` at half 70–130 (3×, side by side) to see
>   *what* a thing is. Use a grid-10 zoom-3 crop only for a single measurement (a wheel's rim, a hoof line).
> - **Read an animal's anatomy before accepting the restyle's figures round it.** On S4b the restyle turned a
>   horse's long dark tail into "a man in blue bending" and its dark lowered head into a second bending man. A tail
>   hangs from the rump to the hocks, and a head rises from the chest. Count the legs under the belly, and give the
>   head point to the mass that joins the neck.
> - **Rider or man behind the animal: test the proportions.** Compare hat-top-to-seat with a seated person's height
>   at that depth (about 0.5 of a standing figure nearby), and standing height with the nearest measured figure.
>   Legs under the belly that reach the hoof line belong to the animal or to someone nearer, not to a man behind it.
> - **Figures whose lower half a cart hides:** estimate the feet from the standing height at that depth (about
>   130–140 px at v ~1000 on S4b). It often puts them on the ground in front of a raised floor, not on it. Say so,
>   and give the other support in the note.
> - **Density guesses come from the scan, not the restyle.** t1's brief guessed 30–35 figures; the scan has 14 in
>   its range. The dense crowd starts at the next tile.
