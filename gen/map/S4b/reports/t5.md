## Lessons (exact text for the skill)

> - **Read trees at zoom 1.2–2 on a clean crop, not at zoom 3 with a 20 px grid.** At zoom 3 the scan's weave hides
>   trunk lines, and hanging willow whips look like slender stems. A crop with tick marks only on its edges (and a
>   faint 50 px grid) shows which strokes join; a CLAHE-enhanced copy (`sharp().clahe()`) brings out faint strokes at
>   the silk's edges. The same crop cut from the mosaic (`u = 12448 + px/2`, `v = py/2` for S4b) gives a side-by-side
>   at any zoom.
> - **Twigs pointing up from a drooping bough are its shoots, not a tree.** Follow the bough's line: a trunk must reach
>   the ground. Upright twigs with no bough above them grow from below and are a tree of their own (its foot hidden).
>   The restyle's "animals" and "pots" in bare branches were budding twigs.
> - **Test a canopy's geometry before calling it a parasol.** Its pole's foot is at about rim v + 2.2 × 77 × the local
>   scale. If that point is visible and shows no pole, the canopy is a roof (or part of one).
> - **A shape over a figure's head** (a "load", a "frame", a "roof") can be a carried rack, a wide hat with a veil, or a
>   stand behind him. Record the figure once and put the readings in its note. Add a prop only if the strokes show the
>   thing's own feet on the ground.
> - **At the silk's top edge over the river, faint strokes may be the far bank** (trees, a shed's posts). Record them
>   with `conf: low` and the support `far bank`, so the river agent's north shore and the build know the far side may
>   be land.
> - **Brief density estimates are not counts.** "About 10–15 on land" was 4 figures here; say so in the report, so
>   nobody hunts for people who are not painted.
