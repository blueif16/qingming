## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **One tree named twice across a seam.** S3's brief put "the big bare tree with the dark trunk" at u 20330–20420 and
  S2's put "the second dark willow trunk" at 20480–20600: on the scan they are one trunk, its foot at u 20465–20600.
  Before giving a seam tree to either side, read the silk's bottom edge on one zoom-0.8 view across the seam, and name
  the tree once in both briefs.
- **Size-check a face before recording it.** A pale patch under an eave at v 1630 read like a profile, but at
  45 × 60 px it was twice a head's size at that v. Compare any doubtful head with a sure figure's head nearby.
- **The restyle adds and removes figures at trunks.** It paints people in knobbed trunks that are only bark (S2 t1,
  t2), and paints bark where a small figure may stand (the top willow at u 20590). Read pale shapes against trunks at
  zoom 2 as well as 3–5: at high zoom the scan breaks into pixels and a figure's outline is lost. Record the doubtful
  ones with `conf: low` and both readings.
- **A contrast-stretched crop helps on dark silk** (under roofs and willow crowns): `sharp(...).normalise({lower: 1,
  upper: 99}).resize(w*z, h*z, {kernel: 'lanczos3'}).sharpen()` with the same grid as `scroll_map overlay`. A
  `--enhance` flag on `scroll_map.mjs overlay` would save every agent writing it (for the lead).
- **zsh doesn't split words:** `for w in "20380-20640 0-300 a"; do set -- $w; …` passes one argument in zsh (the Bash
  tool's shell). Put crop loops in a small bash script in your scratchpad folder.
