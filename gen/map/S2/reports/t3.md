## Lessons (exact text for the skill)

- **A tile's density hint can come from the restyle.** S2's t3 (u 22100–22900) was briefed "medium, about 10–15"; the
  scan has one figure and four trees, and the bank strip's six walkers and stone stair were the restyle's. Count only
  from the scan. An almost empty tile is a valid result: say so in the report, with how the range was walked, so the
  merge doesn't read it as skipped.
- **Use contrast-enhanced crops where the silk is dark.** A greyscale, contrast-stretched crop
  (sharp `greyscale().normalise({ lower: 1, upper: 99 })`, zoom 2–4, grid 10–20) separated a willow's trunk legs from
  the ground wash and a shrub from a lattice where the plain zoom-3 crop was uniform brown. It would be worth an
  `--enhance` flag on `scroll_map.mjs overlay` (S2's t3 used `scratchpad/S2_t3/enh.mjs`).
- **Name the owner of a straddling group's members by entity, not by a u hint.** The trackers were "t2's (u ≈
  21780–22150)", but the rear tracker's foot is at 22173, past t2's range end of 22100, so both units can claim it. t3
  recorded it as a flagged possible duplicate. The brief should say "all five trackers, the easternmost at about 22170,
  are t2's" (or give the boundary by main point).
- **A split-trunk willow** (two legs joined in one knobbed head) is one tree: `base` midway between the legs (est),
  custom points `foot_w` and `foot_e` for each leg's foot, and the two-tree reading in `note`.
