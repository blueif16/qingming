## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **The restyle's registration can drift 30–45 px inside one tile** (S2 t5: the cart's wheel sits at u 24317–24377 in
  the restyle and 24355–24427 on the scan), and where it drifts it also redraws the scene (one animal for two, straw for
  a mat-covered load). Use it for what a thing is, never for where; match it to the scan by a landmark in the same crop
  before reading anything from it.
- **Stretch the contrast instead of zooming further.** Figures at the top of the silk are 50–110 px tall and zoom 3–4
  only magnifies the scan's blur; a gray `normalise` plus a 3 px median, or a mild linear stretch, at zoom 2–3
  separates overlapping table legs, harness lines and heads that the plain crop merges.
- **Count a team's animals by their hooves.** Two sets of hooves at different v (S2: 272 and 297) are two animals, even
  when the near one is foreshortened over the far one.
- **Seated or standing behind a table: compare painted heights** with a standing neighbour at a similar foot v
  (S2: standing about 92 px at v 300, seated men about 60 px at v 180).
- **Read the neighbour's part before writing a seam figure.** When a neighbouring tile has already written its part,
  a figure on the boundary (S2: the man at the horse's head, foot u 24497–24506) is recorded once, on the side the
  neighbour's reading puts the foot, and named in both reports; groups that cross the seam name the neighbour's IDs in
  their `note` for the merge.
