## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **Read faint figures on gridless crops first, then measure on a grid.** At the scan's native resolution, a
  zoom-3 crop with a 10–20 px grid turns 90–130 px figures into texture. A gridless crop at 2.5–3.5× with a light blur
  and a contrast stretch shows the forms (sharp `.blur(1.2).normalise()`, cubic resize). Take the points afterwards
  on a gridded crop of the same window. (S2 t12: the donkey and the bench under the man only showed this way.)
- **Rider or not: check the animal's flank and the seat height.** A rider's leg or robe crosses the animal's flank
  below its back line. Compare his seat-to-head height with the seated figures beside him, and look for a bench under
  his hips. (S2 t12: the "rider" sat on a bench behind the donkey.)
- **Follow a willow "trunk" down to the ground before recording a tree.** In S2's leaning willows, a thick leader
  branch in the crown can look like a trunk. It may belong to a tree whose base is 300 px away, at the silk's edge.
- **The restyle relabels mechanisms as people and the other way round.** S2's bottom boat's rudder head was briefed
  as "a man at its east end"; the donkey was restyled as two men. Settle each on the scan.
- **Tool quirk (zsh):** `for c in "28640 600" …; do set -- $c` does not split words in zsh, so `scroll_inspect.mjs`
  gets NaN. Write the calls out, or use `${=c}`.
