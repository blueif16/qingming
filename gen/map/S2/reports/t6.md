## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **Read identity on greyscale, contrast-stretched crops; read positions on the grid.** The scan has no detail beyond
  zoom 3: the silk is 1800 px tall, and zoom 4–6 shows only weave. At zoom 3, the colour overlay's upsampled weave
  hides faint strokes. A greyscale crop at 2.5–3.5× brings them out: in S2 t6, a horse's lowered head and saddle-cloth
  and a carter's cap showed only there. The crop is made with sharp: `extract` → `greyscale()` →
  `normalise({lower: 2, upper: 98})` → `gamma(1.6)` → `resize` (cubic) → `median(3)`. Then put the points on
  `scroll_map.mjs overlay --grid 20 --zoom 3`.
- **Heads above a roof** (two small capped heads at a ridge's end) can be people behind the building, workers on the
  roof, or ridge ornaments. Record them as figures with conf low, give all three readings, and name them to the
  structures agent, so the merge can settle them on one crop.
- **The restyle turns faint masses of upright strokes into trees.** S2's "willow" at u 24600–24900 is a pole stack, a
  scaffold's pole tips and a striped cloth hung on a pole. Record a tree only where a trunk is painted.
