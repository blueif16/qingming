## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **A straddler the brief assigns to one unit: both units record it under the same ID.** S2's rear tracker had its foot
  at u 22172, in t3's range, while the brief gave all five trackers to t2; t3 recorded it as a safeguard, and t2 used
  t3's ID (`S2.fig.tracker_rear_22172`). `merge` keeps the first part file's copy and lists the duplicate ID, so no
  near-duplicate pair is left to settle by hand. Read the neighbour's part file, if it exists, before naming a
  straddler.
- **The restyle doubles figures with a hunched back or a bundle.** On S2 it made a second tracker with a sack out of a
  bent man's back, and two men out of one man in a broad hat with a rounded back (seven trackers for five). Count heads
  and pairs of legs on the scan, not silhouettes.
- **Heads showing over a moored boat's roof** can stand on the bank behind the boat or on its far-side deck. Put the
  foot `est` where a neighbour's painted height puts it, record the figure (`conf: low`, both readings), and name it in
  the river agent's "left to" line so the merge keeps one.
- **Zooming the scan past 3–4× only blurs it.** For small or faint things use `scroll_inspect.mjs` with half 45–65 (3×,
  restyle beside it); use a zoom-8 overlay only to measure a blot's extent (S2's bird: 16 × 22 px).
