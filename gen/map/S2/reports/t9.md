## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **A brief's density hint is a guess: report the count the strokes give.** S2's t9 was briefed "dense, about 20–25",
  but the scan holds one certain figure. The tables of 喝杯小酒 and the jetty are empty, and the restyle fills
  them. Before trusting a density figure from the setup's first look, check where it came from: the lay of the land
  read at zoom 1 and the restyle both run high.
- **A willow's base can be far from its head.** S2's great willow leans so far that its knobbed head (u 26800–27050)
  is about 200 px of u from its foot (27068, v 905). The setup read the head as the trunk and gave the tree to the
  wrong tile. Follow the trunk down to its roots before assigning a tree by base, and record any second reading (a
  pollard's own short trunk under the head) with its foot point.
- **Frames, trestles and racks standing free in a yard have no owner unless the brief names one.** Give
  "free-standing pole frames, trestles and racks" to the tile agents as props (base corners, the legs' feet in `bays`,
  the apexes as custom points), and say "drop if structures reads it as fixed to a building".
- **When feet are hidden behind a roof, say what the visible part is.** It is head to hip or head to knee. Give the
  foot as a range in the note (S2 t9's porter: v 330–355) and pick a middle value as `est`.
