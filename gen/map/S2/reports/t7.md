## Lessons (exact text for the skill)

- **Find a brief's small groups on a zoom-1 overview first.** The setup agent's positions for minor groups can be a
  couple of hundred px off (S2 t7: "the two standing on the bank" at u 25500–25550 were at 25325–25385).
- **The restyle turns far-street furniture into animals and an animal's head into a man's hat.** On S2's far street it
  drew a horse from a trestle and table legs, an ox head from a bench and a man from a donkey's head. Check every animal
  and figure it shows up the silk for legs, ears and a head on the scan at zoom 3–5, with the restyle beside it.
- **For faint strokes, contrast-stretch the scan crop and stack the restyle under it on the same grid.** A plain
  normalise (sharp `normalise`) at zoom 3–4 reads best; CLAHE amplifies the silk's weave into noise, and zooms above 5
  add no detail on the commons scan.
- **Rooms seen through windows above a lattice skirt are raised floors.** Give the figures `upper floor: <building>`
  with the sill's v and the v of the posts' feet straight below in `note`, so the frame can set the floor's height, and
  tell the structures agent.
