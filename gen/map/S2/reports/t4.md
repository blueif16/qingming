## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **Read small, half-hidden figures on a contrast-stretched grayscale crop at zoom 3–4**, beside the plain colour crop:
  `sharp(...).extract(...).greyscale().normalise({lower: 2, upper: 98}).resize(..., {kernel: 'lanczos3'}).sharpen()`,
  with tick marks only on the margins (a full grid hides the strokes). Above zoom 4 the scan gives noise, not detail.
  On S2's terrace this separated four diners' dark heads from the balustrade and furniture.
- **A dark head is the diner test.** Behind balustrades and in sheds, a mass without a dark head (hair or cap) is more
  often cloth, a bundle or furniture; record it with `conf: low` and both readings, and say the restyle's reading
  separately. The restyle split one leaning diner into two people and turned a lattice screen into a red-robed figure.
- **For seated or leaning figures behind a railing, give the foot from a seated height** (≈ 0.8 of a standing figure's
  painted height at the same v) and write the floor line's v straight below in the note, so the frame can check the
  depth behind the railing.
