## Lessons (exact text for the skill)

- **Read the far street at the silk's top edge at zoom 3 and zoom 5, plain and contrast-stretched.** A crop through
  sharp's `normalise({lower: 1, upper: 99})` with lanczos upscaling (`scratchpad/S2_t8/ecrop.mjs`) shows outlines the
  plain overlay blurs. Zoom 6+ adds only scan noise. On S2's far street a figure is about 53 px tall and drawn with
  1–2 px strokes.
- **A willow's trunk can descend far from its head.** On S2 the great willow by 王家纸马铺 has a knob head sprawling
  250 px along the bank top. Its trunk runs down the bank to roots at the water's edge 230 px lower and 100 px further
  east. Follow the two parallel outlines to the roots before taking the base; the head's west end is not the trunk.
- **A figure on an animal can read as a man walking beside a pale animal.** The restyle drew S2's hooded rider by
  王家纸马铺 that way. On the scan, look for one continuous body line under the figure's lap and the legs hanging at
  the animal's side.
- **Heads showing over a near roof's ridge are figures on the street beyond.** Record them with the foot `est` about
  one figure-height below the head top, and say the roof hides the rest.
