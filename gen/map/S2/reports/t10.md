## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **A grid line can hide a stroke.** A thin vertical stake at u 27900 lay exactly on the 50-px grid line and read as
  grid. When a straight vertical or horizontal stroke falls near a grid value, crop again with `--grid 1000` (no lines)
  before deciding.
- **Zoom 4 adds nothing over zoom 3 on this scan.** For furniture under sheds (tables and benches tangled with rails),
  read the shape at zoom 2–3 with the restyle beside it, accept ±10 px on legs, and mark unclear bench-or-table pieces
  `conf: low` instead of dropping them.
- **The restyle invents children in red beside standing adults.** On S2 east it drew one from a single upright stake
  stroke and one from nothing. Check every small figure in red on the scan at zoom 3.
- **A heap on a unit boundary:** compute the footprint corners' middle before naming the ID, and give a seam note when
  it falls within about 20 px of the boundary. The neighbour may compute it on the other side.
- **Shell:** the Bash tool's shell is zsh, where `set -- $spec` doesn't split words. Write crop loops as a small bash
  script in your scratch folder.
