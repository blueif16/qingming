  `extractChannel('red')` then `normalise()`; script `scratchpad/S7_t1/cropred.mjs`) makes vermilion seals nearly
  as pale as the silk while ink stays dark, so trunks and limbs under a seal can be traced. **A seal's straight
  border is not a trunk:** the restyle painted one there on S7.

  raw zoom-4 crop (`normalise`, low saturation; `scratchpad/S7_t1/crop.mjs`) showed that a pale shape was a sack
  tied at the neck, below the man's sash, and not his robe. Read the points on the gridded crop and the shapes on
  the raw one.

  (S7: from u 930 to u 1232). If no trunk shows between the crown and the ground, it belongs to the nearest trunk,
