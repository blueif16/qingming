## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **Shop-board characters below the scan's resolution:** on the 38414-px scan a board character is about 15 px and
  does not resolve at any zoom. Count the character marks along the board, copy each as □, and put the scholars'
  readings in `note` as readings, not as strokes. Never take characters from the restyle: it paints blobs.
- **A rider on a mostly hidden horse:** settle the horse's facing from the rider and the people at its head (where he
  sits on the visible body, which way his boot in the stirrup points, where the groom stands). Then give fore, hind
  and head `est` from a body length of about one standing figure's height. Write which strokes were taken for the
  horse and which readings were rejected (S7: a pale outlined oval was the hindquarters, not a load or a horse facing
  the other way).
- **The restyle turns objects into people:** at S7's well it painted a hooped bucket as a child, and in the shop it
  added a second child. Hoops, bails and lids on the scan decide. A person the scholars expect but the strokes only
  hint at gets its own entity with `conf: low` and both readings.
- **Contrast-normalised crops for faint figures:** `sharp(...).normalise()` at zoom 3 separates a faint child's face
  from robe folds better than the plain scan or the restyle. CLAHE amplifies the silk weave and hides it. Above
  zoom 4 the scan only blurs.
- **A lead's density estimate is not a quota:** S7 t2 was estimated at 20–25 figures and the strokes have 12. Walk
  the range twice and report the honest count.
