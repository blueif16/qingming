## Lessons (exact text for the skill)

- **Faded silk reads best at zoom 2 with each channel normalised, not at zoom 3–4.** Where the silk is dark and faded
  (S1), raw crops above zoom 3 break into the silk's weave. Read points at zoom 1.8–2.5 on a crop normalised per channel
  (sharp `normalise({lower: 0.5, upper: 99.5})`), and orient at zoom 1. For tiny far figures, a light blur (σ ≈ 1.2)
  followed by a greyscale normalise separates ink from weave.
- **Follow every dark band to its ends before counting animals.** The restyle read the foot of a willow trunk leaning
  behind a lying buffalo as a second buffalo's head (S1 29300). On the scan the band runs on, unbroken, up into the
  trunk and the crown.
- **Measure "rows of small plants" against a figure before leaving them to the field.** S1's 菜地 "rows" have trunks
  90–180 px tall beside 145 px adults: they are 13 young trees (the tiles' trees), not crops.
- **In the agents' zsh, `set -- $var` does not split words.** Run multi-crop loops under `bash -c '…'`.
