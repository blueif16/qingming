## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **Worn silk: read at zoom 1.3–2 with a contrast stretch, not zoom 3+.** In S1's farmyard the weave is coarser than
  the strokes, and zoom 3–5 dissolves them into texture. To separate overlapping trunks in brush, take a column-darkness
  profile: the mean luminance per column over a 15–20 row band, at several v. Each dark run is a trunk edge, and pairs
  give each trunk's width and centre. The foot is where the pairs stop.
- **Check that the silk under a restyle figure is not plain before reading it as faint.** In S1 east, the restyle put
  a man with a loaded donkey, two standing men and a thick willow trunk on silk that is plain at every contrast. A
  crease line running the full height of the silk can sit where it draws a figure.
- **Crow's nests on the bare trees at the scroll's start** are dark tangled clumps at forks. They go in the tree's
  `what` with a point in `note`; the builder may want them, so the merge should decide whether they become entities.
- **Tool quirk:** `node tools/scroll_map.mjs <S> overlay --help` is not help. It renders the whole section to
  `gen/map/<S>/overlay_<u0>_<u1>.jpg`, inside the area's folder. Always pass `--out` into your scratch folder (I moved
  the stray file out).
