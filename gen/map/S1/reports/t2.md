## Lessons (exact text for the skill)

- "**Read faint ink-wash figures on a grey high-pass crop**: the scan minus a 12 px Gaussian blur, ×4, offset to mid
  grey, at zoom 2.5–5 with the grid. On S1, contrast stretching left the travellers and the party as smudges. The
  high-pass crop showed the donkeys' eyes and ears, the sedan's poles, the porters' loads and which way each animal
  faces. Keep the script (about 20 lines of sharp) in your scratch folder."
- "**Settle each animal's facing on the strokes before trusting the vignette's story.** On S1 the party's horse and
  both travellers' donkeys face east, away from the city, though the scholarship reads both groups as going home.
  Record what is painted, and give the other reading in the note."
- "**When the restyle's tile and its overview disagree about a faint group, both are inventing.** At S1's party rider
  the tile draws three men and the overview a rider with a groom. Count legs, hooves and torsos on the strokes: four
  hooves and one torso make one horse and its rider."
- "**`head` goes on the head itself.** When something round sits behind or on a head (a hat blown back, a bundle on the
  shoulders), give its extents and both readings in the note."
- "**Named owners and range owners can split a group at a range edge.** On S1, t2 named the sedan party but one
  porter's feet were at 31130, in t3's range. Give the stray member's points in the group's note and the report, so the
  merge adds him."
