  on the plain scan. At zoom 3 the faint far trunks dissolve into the silk weave; at zoom 1.6–2.6 with contrast, every
  stem reads. Two stems whose lines converge below the hiding crown are one tree; a stem that joins another tree's
  crown is its branch."
- "When every far trunk is hidden at the foot, take the foot line from the trunks of the same grove that show the
  lowest end (S1: three trunks ending at v 640–657) and put every hidden base of that grove on it, `est`, with the
  method in `note`. Say which tree looks too big on that line (S1 `far_bare_elm_32510`, 485 px)."
- "Interlaced pollard willows: assign whips only near the heads, mark the crown top and extremes `est`, and give a
  high fan with no head of its own to the tree whose head it rises from. Two boles that touch are two trees when
  each has its own foot on the ground and its own pollard head. Read the feet where the pale ground shows under
  each (S1: 155 px of v apart)."
