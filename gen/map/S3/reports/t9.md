## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **Contrast-stretch faint outlines, don't zoom past 3.** The scan is 1800 px tall, so zoom 4 and up only blurs. On
  outline-only figures over darkened silk (S3's south-bank shop), a crop with a 1–99 % levels stretch and a light
  sharpen at zoom 2.2–3 shows strokes the plain overlay hides. Read at zoom 2–2.6 for what a thing is, then measure at
  zoom 3. (Proposed for the lead: a `--stretch` option on `scroll_map.mjs overlay`.)
- **Measure a big tree's base before deciding its owner.** A setup's position for a tree is often where the trunk
  first shows, not where it meets the ground. S3's dark-trunk tree, briefed at 20330–20420, has its base at the silk's
  bottom edge at ≈ 20525, past the seam.
- **One trunk can change tone along its length.** S3's dark-trunk tree is dark and mottled below and pale above, where
  a straight broken limb crosses a roof and a boat. Join the parts by their outlines, not their tone, and say so in
  the note.
- **Empty furniture is painted furniture.** A 名画记 "tavern" pin doesn't mean customers are painted. Record every table,
  bench and chair as a prop even when nobody sits at it, and say in the report that the tables are empty, so nobody
  casts diners there.
