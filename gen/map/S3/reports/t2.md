## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **Far rows at the silk's top edge are at the scan's resolution limit** (S3: figures about 30 px). Read them at
  zoom 3. Zoom 4–6 only blurs, and contrast-limited enhancement (CLAHE) turns silk texture into false strokes. A
  plain normalise (contrast stretch) at zoom 2–2.5 separates trunks from willow whips best. Where the restyle
  registers well, use it to name what a trunk does (its lean), never where it stands.
- **A pollarded willow's knot is its head, not its foot.** On S3 the far-bank willows radiate whips in every direction
  from a knobby head halfway up the crown. The trunk leans from the bank to the head. Put `base` where the whips and
  the trunk converge at the bank (`est` when a boat or a roof hides it), and say where the head is in `what`.
- **Tracker files:** count the men head by head and follow the line to the man it ends at. Give the line's two ends in
  the rear man's note, so the river agent can name him as the rope's far end.
