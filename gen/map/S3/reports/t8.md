## Lessons (exact text for the skill, "Mapping a section")

- **Over dense crowds the scan's resolution is the limit.** Zoom 3.5 and beyond shows only grain. Read the crowd at zoom
  2–2.6 with a contrast stretch (sharp `normalise()` plus `linear(1.15, -15)` on the crop), count heads there, and use zoom 3
  only to place the points. A shape that reads as a second head only at zoom 3 (a raised arm curling beside a head) is
  usually part of its neighbour: check the gestalt at zoom 2 before adding a figure.
- **Railing crowds on an arched deck:** feet are hidden, so put each foot just behind the railing's foot line (the deck's
  near edge) at the figure's own u, `est`; where legs show between the rails, use them. Write the railing's foot v every
  50–100 px in the report so the bridge agent can compare it with its deck edge.
- **Figures behind roofs and sheds:** give the foot `est` from the painted height of a whole figure nearby, and say what
  hides it. The foot then lies inside the roof's area on the scan; that is correct, not a misplacement.
- **Use the neighbours' painted heights to choose between readings.** At the silk's top edge the far figures are ~80 px
  tall; a "man" 100 px tall among them is more likely a stand or a load in front of a smaller man.
- **Things at a moored boat's end:** a figure overlapping a hull's band can be crew or a person on the bank. Record it with
  `conf: low` and all readings, and name it in the river agent's "left to" so one of the two is dropped at the merge.
