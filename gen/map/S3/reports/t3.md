## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **Stretch the contrast before you read dark silk.** On S3's tavern gate the plain zoom-3 overlay crops were too dark
  and soft to read figures. A crop through sharp `normalise({lower: 1, upper: 99})`, lanczos upscaling and `sharpen()`,
  with the same scan-px grid, showed the horse's tack, the parasol's ribs and the far bank's seated men. Past zoom 4 the
  scan's texture swamps the strokes; zoom 2–2.5 often reads a figure's whole outline better than zoom 4.
- **A "rider" in a brief is a hint, not a fact.** At 十千脚店's gate the brief's rider is a saddled horse with an empty
  saddle. Read the saddle before you cast a rider.
- **Read a cart's front from its harness end.** On a 串车 the end with the raised poles, a crossbar, and ropes with
  loops and hooks is the front (where the animal or the front man takes it). The end whose poles rest on the ground
  beside rest legs is the rear (the handles), even when men stand there. Say both ends' points in the report, so the
  neighbour on the other side of the boundary can check the reading.
- **When the restyle drifts, trust it for *what* and never for *where*.** Over the far bank at u 17050–17400 the
  restyle moved the willows about 60 px east but not the seated figures. At the gate it doubled one figure into two and
  turned a lattice panel into a person in red.
