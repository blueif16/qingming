## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **Read a dense crowd on a contrast-stretched crop at zoom 2.2–2.8, not zoom 3+.** On this scan (1800 px tall) zoom 3+
  dissolves strokes into the silk's weave. A crop run through `sharp(...).normalise({ lower: 1, upper: 99 }).sharpen()`
  with a light 20 px grid labelled on all four sides made heads, hats and feet readable that the plain overlay blurred.
  The script is `scratchpad/S3_t5/crop.mjs`, args `u0-u1 v0-v1 zoom out.jpg [points.json]`.
- **Plot your candidate points on the crop before you write the part.** A list of `[label, u, v]` drawn on the
  contrast crop shows 25 heads at once and catches two marks on one person or a load read as a head before the
  overlay step.
- **Follow the ropes before counting a head.** A dark jar in a rope sling at a porter's pole end looks exactly like a
  dark-haired head at zoom 2 (S3, (18385, 900)). So does a wrapped bundle over a man's shoulders.
- **A footprint prop's main point is its `sw` corner** (`scroll_map.mjs` `primary()` takes the first ground point,
  and that is sw), not its centre. Decide the ownership of a stall with base corners by its sw corner.
- **Read a neighbour's part before finishing, when it exists.** On the t4|t5 boundary both tiles had read the same
  man and the same lattice awning. Dropping the second copy before the merge leaves the boundary clean, and the
  neighbour's notes settled a donkey's facing and rope.
- **Ask the bridge agent's geometry before labelling supports at a bridge's foot.** The deck's far edge may not be
  painted. S3's bridge agent put it 750 px west of the near edge, along the water, so a crowd that looks like the
  bank's (no ramp edge drawn) falls on the ramp at H 1–3 m. Label by the bridge agent's feet, and put the other
  reading and the deck_e point at the same v in the note. A deck that is level across the picture needs H by v, not
  by the entity's u.
- **Over a section where the restyle is misregistered** (here 50–100 px, recomposed), don't use its tiles even to name
  things. The overview names a few masses (a robe, a bundle on a pole). Read everything else from the strokes.
