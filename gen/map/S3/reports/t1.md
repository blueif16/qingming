## Lessons (exact text for the skill `scroll-section`, "Mapping a section")

- **Check a seam's agreed extents on one wide crop across the seam before trusting them.** On S3, t1 found S4b's
  biggest rafted boat running on to a bow at u ≈ 15790, 340 px past the "bow ≈ 15450" in the seam table. A boat
  half-hidden behind a tree is easy to cut short.
- **When a mass's size doesn't fit what it would be,** record both things with `conf: low` and each note naming the
  other's reading, so the merge can drop one without re-reading the scroll. On S3 a 58 px heap on a table, with
  heads 18–25 px, is either food or a diner's flowered head, over either a diner's back or a basin.
- **Crowns whose trunks are hidden** (behind a ridge, below the silk's edge): one tree per fan of shoots that opens
  from a hidden centre. Where two fans overlap, their inner shoots cross in an X; that X marks the gap between two
  bases. Write the possible v range of the hidden base in the note.
- **Two derived crops read faint figures and twigs better than the plain zoom-3 crop:**
  - a contrast-stretched greyscale crop (sharp: `greyscale().normalise({lower:2, upper:98}).linear(1.4, -40)`, with
    a 20 px grid drawn in SVG);
  - a colour crop with saturation raised (`modulate({saturation: 1.6})`), which shows the few red touches that tell
    food or flowers from a robe.

  On S3 t1 these settled the diners and showed there is no red figure where the restyle paints one. They could
  become a mode of `tools/scroll_map.mjs overlay` (the lead's call).
