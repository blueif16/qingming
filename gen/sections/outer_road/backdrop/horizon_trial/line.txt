Everything in this picture is far away: its nearest things stand some seventy paces from us, so even the nearest people are small, about a twentieth of the image height, with their heads on the horizon and their feet just under it. The painted ground under the horizon is no deeper than those people are tall, and the road is a flat sliver of it, already narrow at its near end, never a wide wedge opening toward the bottom of the image. Below that sliver of ground there is only white mist.
