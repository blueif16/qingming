PART: sprays of narrow pointed bamboo leaves
WHAT: bamboo leaf sprays
EACH: Each one is a single spray of narrow pointed bamboo leaves on a few thin green-grey stalks that spring from one point at the bottom centre of its quarter: the leaves drawn in clusters of three to five strokes like the character 个, layered densely in deep green (about #4e6b4a) and fresher green (about #6f8c58), never yellow. There is no culm below the point. The spray fills most of its quarter.
SHAPES: (1) an upright spray; (2) a wide spray drooping a little at both sides; (3) a spray leaning to the left; (4) a spray leaning to the right.
