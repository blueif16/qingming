PART: hanging bare whips with their first specks of green
WHAT: curtains of hanging willow whips
EACH: Each one is a single loose curtain of long, thin, flexible bare whips that all hang from one small point at the TOP centre of its quarter and fall straight down like strings, spreading a little as they fall, yellow-green to red-brown (about #a6a46a), with tiny swelling buds and a few first specks of pale willow green (about #a3b56e). See-through, with plenty of white between the strands. There is no branch above the point. The curtain fills most of the height of its quarter.
SHAPES: (1) a narrow curtain falling straight; (2) a wide curtain spreading as it falls; (3) a curtain swaying a little to the left; (4) a curtain swaying a little to the right.
