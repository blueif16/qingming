PART: long straight whips with their tiny new leaves
WHAT: sprays of willow whips
EACH: Each one is a single dense spray of many long, straight, slender red-brown whips that all spring from one small point at the bottom centre of its quarter and rise upward and outward like a fountain, the longest arching over a little at their tips, with tiny pale fresh-green new leaves (about #8fa35f) just opening along them. The whips are single unbranched lines from that point; there is no knob, no branch and no trunk below the point. The spray fills most of its quarter.
SHAPES: (1) a tall upright fountain, narrow at the bottom and wide at the top; (2) a wide dome of whips arching out on both sides; (3) a spray sweeping far out to the left; (4) a spray sweeping far out to the right.
