PART: fans of needle strokes
WHAT: fans of pine needles
EACH: Each one is a single loose, see-through fan of fine, straight needle strokes, muted blue-green (about #5a7462) with a lighter green on the upper strokes: a half-circle of separate lines radiating from one point at the bottom centre of its quarter, like a half-open folding fan, with white showing between the strokes. Never a solid pad, never a clipped cloud shape. There is no branch and no trunk below the point. The fan fills most of its quarter.
SHAPES: (1) an upright half-open fan; (2) a wide fan spreading almost flat; (3) a fan tilted to the left; (4) a fan tilted to the right.
