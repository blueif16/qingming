PART: long straight shoots with their tiny buds
WHAT: fans of willow shoots
EACH: Each one is a single fan of many long, straight, stiff, slender red-brown shoots that all spring from one small point at the bottom centre of its quarter and rise upward and outward like the ribs of a half-open folding fan, arching over a little at their tips, with tiny pale fresh-green buds (about #8fa35f) along them. The shoots are single unbranched lines from that point; there is no knob, no branch and no trunk below the point. The fan fills most of its quarter.
SHAPES: (1) a tall narrow upright fan; (2) a wide half-open fan spreading almost level at its sides; (3) a fan leaning to the left; (4) a fan leaning to the right.
