PART: thin upright shoots and twigs with their buds
WHAT: sprays of young bare shoots
EACH: Each one is a single airy spray of many long, thin, straight shoots that spring from one small point at the bottom centre of its quarter and rise steeply and fan out, each branching once or twice into finer upright twigs, red-brown to grey-brown, the tips carrying tiny dark swelling buds and a few fresh pale green specks (about #93a866). There is no trunk below the point. The spray fills most of its quarter.
SHAPES: (1) a tall narrow vase-shaped spray; (2) a wide spray, as wide as tall; (3) a spray leaning to the left; (4) a spray leaning to the right.
