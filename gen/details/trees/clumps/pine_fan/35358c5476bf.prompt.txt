Image 1 shows the target art style. Image 2 is a tree painted in that style; copy exactly how its fans of needle strokes are drawn: the same stroke, line weight, colours and spacing.

Paint a sheet of four separate fans of pine needles, arranged in a 2 by 2 grid, each one alone in its own quarter of the picture with clear white space all round it, never touching another one or the picture's edge.

Each one is a single loose, see-through fan of fine, straight needle strokes, muted blue-green (about #5a7462) with a lighter green on the upper strokes: a half-circle of separate lines radiating from one point at the bottom centre of its quarter, like a half-open folding fan, with white showing between the strokes. Never a solid pad, never a clipped cloud shape. There is no branch and no trunk below the point. The fan fills most of its quarter.

The four differ only in shape: (1) an upright half-open fan; (2) a wide fan spreading almost flat; (3) a fan tilted to the left; (4) a fan tilted to the right.

True natural early-spring daylight colours of the real tree, never the yellowed silk of the scroll. Isolated on a solid plain pure white background that fills the whole picture edge to edge: no trunk, no branch, no ground, no shadow, no text, no seal, no grid lines, no border.
