Image 1 shows the target art style. Image 2 is a tree painted in that style; copy exactly how its sprays of narrow pointed bamboo leaves are drawn: the same stroke, line weight, colours and spacing.

Paint a sheet of four separate bamboo leaf sprays, arranged in a 2 by 2 grid, each one alone in its own quarter of the picture with clear white space all round it, never touching another one or the picture's edge.

Each one is a single spray of narrow pointed bamboo leaves on a few thin green-grey stalks that spring from one point at the bottom centre of its quarter: the leaves drawn in clusters of three to five strokes like the character 个, layered densely in deep green (about #4e6b4a) and fresher green (about #6f8c58), never yellow. There is no culm below the point. The spray fills most of its quarter.

The four differ only in shape: (1) an upright spray; (2) a wide spray drooping a little at both sides; (3) a spray leaning to the left; (4) a spray leaning to the right.

True natural early-spring daylight colours of the real tree, never the yellowed silk of the scroll. Isolated on a solid plain pure white background that fills the whole picture edge to edge: no trunk, no branch, no ground, no shadow, no text, no seal, no grid lines, no border.
