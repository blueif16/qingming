## Build

1. **The builder** `blender/<folder>_scene.py` the way `blender/outer_road_scene.py` and `inner_city_scene.py` are
   written: `derive()` from the layout; every piece inside `sb.entity(<map id>)` (strict buckets); `choice:` for every
   size the strokes can't give; nothing that isn't in the map; one invisible wall per side of the walkable area
   (`edge:south|east|north|west`), plus `edge:shore` along every shore. Real size, painted place (rule 5): far rows keep
   real heights and are narrowed, never shrunk; where there is no room to walk (the far bank and the north-bank strip
   in S3 and S2 are painted at 0.2–0.7 scale), make room with recorded moves in `build_choices.json`, each with its
   reason, not by changing the frame. New parts go in your own `blender/kit_<folder>.py` as generic functions; shared
   kits (`song_kit.py`, `kit_vehicles.py`, `kit_boats.py`, `kit_rainbow_bridge.py`) are imported, never edited: ask
   the lead.
2. **Ground at the seams:** your ground stops 0.5 m past a seam with an older neighbour and dips 0.12 m under it; at an
   outer edge it runs 16 m out under the mist. The lead cuts the older section's ground back when you join.
3. **Water and boats** (S4b, S3, S2; S1's stream): one water strip per reach from `kit_boats.water_strip()` (until the
   page agent delivers it, a stub with the same signature), banks between shore and bank top; every boat from
   `kit_boats.boat_from_layout()` exported in your boats GLB (`export_web_scene.mjs`'s boats option), its crew in
   people.json by the crew schema in `boats_page_lessons.md`. The water level is your frame's `supports.water`.
4. **Set pieces:** S3 builds the Rainbow Bridge with `kit_rainbow_bridge.build()` from its layout entity and frame (the
   deck's `H_profile_X`), and its 彩楼欢门 scaffold as part of 十千脚店 in its own kit; S2 its 望火楼; S1 its thatched farmhouses, threshing floor, fences and fields. If the catalogue sends a set piece to a
   factory, build against its stub signature and swap when it lands.
5. **Headless only:** `tools/build_scene.sh <folder>` (it waits for one of the machine's three Blender slots); any other
   headless Blender run goes through `tools/blender_slot.sh`. Never the live GUI.
6. **Export:** `node tools/export_web_scene.mjs <folder>` (and the boats GLB).
