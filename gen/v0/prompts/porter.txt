Character design turnaround for a stylized low-poly 3D game. A Northern Song dynasty porter: adult man, slim wiry build, about 6.5 heads tall, tanned skin, simple small face with two dark dot eyes and short brows, tiny nose, no detailed mouth. Hair in a small topknot wrapped with an off-white cloth head-wrap. Short undyed hemp jacket with crossed collar closing to the right, tied at the waist with a cloth sash, sleeves rolled to the forearm. Loose faded-indigo trousers rolled up to just below the knee, bare shins, straw sandals. Neutral standing pose, empty hands. Shapes simple enough for a model of about 3000 triangles.
Art style: stylized low-poly real-time 3D game render, simple clean readable shapes, flat colour fills with only one soft darker shadow tone, no surface textures, no PBR, no photorealism. Thin dark ink-brown outlines around shapes and along major folds, like a hand-inked illustration. Muted palette taken from an aged Northern Song painting on silk: warm silk ochre, pale beige plaster, ink-brown timber, soft grey-blue clay roof tiles, undyed hemp white, faded indigo, dull vermilion and malachite-green accents, sage-green willow. Calm, airy, cozy indie-game look. Plain flat mid-grey background, no props, no text.
