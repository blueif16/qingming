The tree: an old willow (柳) in early spring as the scroll paints it at the scroll's end, the only willow in the painting whose whips hang. A gnarled, lumpy, knotted trunk with rough grey-brown bark rises and divides into a few crooked limbs; from the limbs and the upper trunk hang long, thin, flexible bare whips in loose curtains, falling straight down like strings, the way a willow's twigs hang before its leaves open. Early spring: the whips are bare, with only tiny swelling buds and the first specks of pale green along them; no leaf masses, no full foliage, no summer green crown; the curtains are see-through, with plenty of sky between the strands.
Colours: grey-brown bark with darker knots; the whips yellow-green to red-brown (about #a6a46a); buds and the first tiny leaves pale willow green (about #a3b56e), sparse.
