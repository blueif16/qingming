The animal is a Bactrian camel of a Northern Song caravan from the north-west: two firm humps, a long neck curving down and up, a small head with a split upper lip and small rounded ears, long legs with broad two-toed padded feet, a short thin tail. Sandy fawn coat, with long shaggy darker brown hair on the throat (a beard), the front of the neck, the top of the head, the hump tops and the upper forelegs. About 1.75 m at the back between the humps. Simple clean shapes, the shaggy hair as a few big soft locks, no fine fur detail.
