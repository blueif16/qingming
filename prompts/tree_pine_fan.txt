The tree: a pine-like tree as the scroll paints it, its needles drawn as open fans of strokes. A twisting, knotty trunk with grey-brown bark scarred by dark knots leans and bends into a few crooked limbs. Along the limbs and at their ends sit loose, see-through fans of fine, straight needle strokes: each fan a half-circle of separate lines radiating from one point, like a half-open folding fan, with sky showing between the strokes. The fans are irregular and scattered, never solid pads, never clipped cloud shapes, never a bonsai or a garden pine.
Colours: grey-brown bark with dark knots; needles muted blue-green (about #5a7462) with a lighter green on the upper fans; no yellow.
