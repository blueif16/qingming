The tree: a bare elm (榆) in early spring as the scroll paints it, before any leaves. A gnarled, knotted grey-brown trunk with a few paler bark patches and dark hollows twists up and splits into crooked limbs. The crown is a ragged, dense mass of dark, angular "crab-claw" twigs: short, bent, knobbly twigs that hook and turn like claws, drawn in dark ink, ending in small dark clumps of tight buds. No leaves, no blossom.
Colours: grey-brown trunk with a few paler grey patches and dark knots; the limbs darker grey-brown and the twigs dark brown-grey, darkest toward their tips; buds dark brown, never pink or red.
