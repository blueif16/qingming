looking east out of the town along a country road: the next stretch of the scroll (image 2), seen from its west end at eye level. Image 2's left end is nearest to us and its right end farthest away; its top edge is on our left and its bottom edge on our right. All of it is far off across a stretch of white mist, so even its nearest sheds, animals and people are small, the people's heads on the horizon. The ground lies flat at the horizon as a thin strip; below it there is only white mist. Nearest of all, on the right, one massive old pollard willow with a dark knobbly bole spreads a wide fan of shoots. Then, on the left of the middle: a village stall, a low open-fronted shed under a thatched hip roof with a lower thatched end, a man at its counter and a customer, a water buffalo lying in front of it and an ox standing with its head lowered; behind it a small thatched shed, and three pollard willows standing over the stall. Beside it a small vegetable field in low banked steps, planted with a little grove of young bare trees, thin dark stems with small crab-claw twig crowns, and a wheelbarrow tipped over on the ground. Then a tiled farmhouse with a veranda and a board-walled front wing; on the road before it travellers riding small donkeys, a traveller with a staff, grooms running after a bolting horse. Farther: a low thatched hut behind tall wattle fences, under one huge old willow whose low hollow trunk leans hard over the hut; an open shed on posts; on a track coming down toward us a party returning from the tombs: a tall closed dark sedan chair carried by two bearers, a big spray of long willow twigs fanning up from its roof, a rider on a horse and porters with shoulder poles and boxes. Farthest, on the right: a long low dyke lined with great old pollard willows, massive knobbly boles with roots like rocks, and beyond them a few bare elms. The willows are pollard willows before their leaves open: from each knobbly head a half-open fan of long, straight, stiff bare shoots springs upward and outward. They are NOT weeping willows: no hanging curtains, no drooping strands, no foliage. Early spring: bare branches, no blossom, no green fields. No river, no boats, no bridge, no city, no walls, no towers, no hills or mountains, no lake.
