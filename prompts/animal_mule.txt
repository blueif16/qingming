The animal is a strong Chinese mule of the Northern Song that pulls the four-mule freight wagons: horse-sized, about 1.35 m at the withers, a solid body, a straight head with long upright donkey-like ears, a short upright mane, a thin tail with long hair from halfway down, slim legs with small hooves. Dark bay-brown coat, a paler tan muzzle, eye rings and belly, dark lower legs and hooves. Simple clean shapes, no fine fur detail.
