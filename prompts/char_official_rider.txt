The character is a Song official on his way home with his retinue: a dignified middle-aged man, about 7 heads tall, a long calm face with dark dot eyes, a long black moustache and a full black beard to the chest. A black lacquered gauze hat with a tall flat-topped crown and a very broad flat round brim, much wider than his shoulders, two black ties hanging from it. A pale off-white round-collared long robe (圆领袍) reaching to the ankles, wide sleeves to the wrist, belted at the waist with a black leather belt; black cloth boots.
