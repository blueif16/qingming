Image 1 is the target art style. Paint a sheet of sixteen separate dressed-stone block faces in exactly image 1's style (hand-inked dark-brown linework, light hatching, muted painted colours, aged-silk palette). Flat, front-on, no perspective, no cast shadows, no text.

A 4x4 grid of sixteen square cells with thin pale gaps between them. Each cell is filled edge to edge by one stone face seen straight on. Each stone face has a soft painted centre with faint chisel marks, and a hand-inked, irregular outline just inside the cell edge: worn, rounded, slightly chipped edges, darkened with a little hatching, so the block looks cut by hand and never like a perfect rectangle.

The sixteen stones all differ: light grey, warm grey, buff, pale ochre, grey-brown; a few with a faint green or rust stain, one or two with a hairline crack, a couple worn more on one edge. Mid tones only, never white, never black.
