The tree: a huge old pollarded willow as the scroll paints it. A massive, short, swollen bole, dark and mottled, lumpy with burls, knots and hollows, its foot flaring into a few exposed roots. The bole rises to a knobbly, clubbed pollard head (where the tree was cut back year after year), and from that head a dense fountain of long, straight, slender whips springs upward and outward, arching a little at the tips, the longest sweeping far out to one side. The whips grow straight from the head; they are long single shoots, not branches, and they do not hang in curtains. Tiny new leaves are just opening along them.
Colours: dark grey-brown bark mottled with paler grey patches and a few spots of moss green; whips red-brown; new leaves fresh pale willow green (about #8fa35f).
