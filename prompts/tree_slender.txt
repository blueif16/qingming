The tree: a slender bare tree as the scroll paints it between the houses: a thin, tall trunk with dark grey-brown bark and a few knots, bare of branches for most of its height, with only a few thin bare branches near the top. Early spring: no leaves, only tiny buds.
Colours: dark grey-brown bark with darker knots; twigs dark grey-brown; buds dark, with only specks of pale green (about #a3b56e).
