The plant: a clump of bamboo as the scroll paints it in the courtyards: a dozen thin, straight, jointed culms rising close together, the upper culms bending slightly, with sprays of narrow pointed leaves drawn in clusters of three to five strokes (like the character 个) along their upper two-thirds, layered densely in darker and lighter greens.
Colours: culms green-grey with darker joints; leaves deep green (about #4e6b4a) and fresher green (about #6f8c58), never yellow.
