The character is a Song boat tracker (纤夫) who hauls the grain boats upstream on a tow rope: a wiry, hard-working adult man, about 6.5 heads tall, a lean sunburnt face with dark dot eyes and short brows, clean shaven, black hair under a small round brown cloth cap that fits close over the crown. A short faded khaki-tan hemp jacket with a crossed collar to the hips, sleeves to the wrist, belted tight at the waist with a twisted rope; loose dark grey trousers rolled up to below the knee, bare shins and woven straw sandals.
