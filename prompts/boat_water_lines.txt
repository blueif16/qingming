A seamless, tileable square texture of the current lines of a river, drawn the way the Song dynasty handscroll
"Along the River During the Qingming Festival" draws the fierce current of the Bian river: thin, fine ink lines of
even weight, in dark grey ink on plain white. The lines run left to right, roughly parallel, about twelve to sixteen
of them across the square, each a long, gently undulating stroke that swells and narrows slightly, breaks off, and
starts again a little offset; here and there two strokes close into a small eye-shaped eddy or a tight curl, as the
painting does. Loose, hand-drawn, calm, sparse: more white than ink. The pattern continues across all four edges so
the tile repeats with no seam.
Nothing else: no boats, no banks, no shading, no wash, no colour, no paper or silk texture, no border, no signature,
no seals, no text. Pure white background, only the ink lines.
